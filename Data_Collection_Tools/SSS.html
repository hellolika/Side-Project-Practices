Dataset Engineering & Frame Sampling Simulator

Extracting every consecutive frame (30 fps) from a video leads to severe overfitting and data leakage during deepfake detector training. International computer vision benchmarks (FaceForensics++, Celeb-DF v2, KoDF, and FMFCC-V) rely on uniform temporal sampling (10 to 50 frames per video). Use this interactive calculator to adjust sampling density, compute required download volumes, estimate storage footprints, and assess generalization risk.

                        ⚙️ RetinaFace Bounding-Box Protocol
                    

                    
    
                        
  • 
                            1.
                            Landmark Alignment: Detect 5 facial landmarks (eyes, nose, mouth corners) using RetinaFace to geometrically align roll and tilt before cropping.
                        
    • 
                        
  • 
                            2.
                            1.3× Box Expansion: Expand raw face bounding boxes by 1.3× to capture outer jawline, chin, and forehead boundaries where deepfake boundary artifacts occur.
                        
    • 
                        
  • 
                            3.
                            Standard Resizing: Resize all face crops to standard 224 × 224 pixels PNG format for model ingestion.
                        
    • 
                        
  • 
                            4.
                            Confidence Thresholding: Discard crops with detection confidence < 0.95 or width < 120 pixels to filter out low-res crowd backgrounds.

                            🚀 Overview & Objectives
                        

                        

                            To train robust deepfake detectors capable of safeguarding Cambodian public communications, engineers 
                            require a balanced dataset of Cambodian facial images. Manual searching is time-consuming and inefficient. 
                            This tool provides a centralized, interactive control center to streamline the acquisition of high-quality face images of Cambodian individuals 
                            from official and public broadcast sources. Rather than performing manual video searches, 
                            by this, allows teams to explore pre-verified YouTube seed links, simulate frame sampling parameters,

                            So, this interactive control panel eliminates manual search overhead by curating 73 verified YouTube links.

Module-by-Module User Guide

                    

                        

                            

                                🔗 1. Seed Link Explorer
                            

                            
Filter all 73 verified seed links by category (Gov Press, Ministry, News). Search titles or channels, and use the Copy Filtered URLs button to feed your Python ingestion pipeline.

                        


                        

                            

                                📊 2. Sampling Simulator
                            

                            
Adjust sliders for dataset goals and sampling rate. Automatically computes required videos count, raw 1080p bandwidth, and 224x224 crop storage footprint to avoid data leakage.

                        


                        

                            

                                📺 3. Channel Directory
                            

                            
Access official master channel upload playlists (`@tvkcambodia`, RGSU, Ministry channels) for automated bulk scraping of entire video archives.

                        


                        

                            

                                ⚡ 4. Command Generator
                            

                            
Select target video formats and duration caps to output copy-paste `yt-dlp` bash scripts pre-configured with title exclusions for sports and TV series.

                        


                        

                            

                                ⚙️ 5. CV Cropping Standard
                            

                            
Step-by-step specifications for 5-landmark alignment, 1.3× bounding-box expansion, and 224×224 pixel normalization for RetinaFace detectors.

                        


                        

                            

                                ⚖️ 6. Legal & Regulatory
                            

                            
Compliance checklist ensuring alignment with Sub-Decree No. 252 (2021) and MPTC Cybersecurity Directives for public domain government research.
